## Hi, I'm Baoyue Huang 👋

PhD student in **Space Physics** at the **University of Texas at Dallas**, advised by **Prof. Lunjin Chen**.

I study **lightning-generated whistlers (LGWs)** in Earth's inner magnetosphere, combining **spacecraft observations** and **wave propagation simulations**.

### Research

- **Whistler dispersion as a density probe**: I fit LGW dispersion observed by the **Van Allen Probes (RBSP) / EMFISIS** to relate the dispersion (D) to the magnetospheric **electron density (nₑ)**.
- **Lightning source matching**: I use **WWLLN** (World Wide Lightning Location Network) lightning data to identify the source strokes of whistlers seen in space.
- **Observed spatial dependence**: I examine how the observed dispersion varies with **L-shell** and **magnetic latitude**.
- **Whistler propagation simulation**: Using an existing whistler propagation model, I run simulations to study how dispersion depends on L-shell and latitude, and how it differs between **ducted and non-ducted** propagation.
- **Observation vs. simulation**: I use simulations to reproduce the whistlers observed by RBSP and evaluate how well the simulations match the observations.
