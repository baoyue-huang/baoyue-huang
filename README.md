# Hi, I'm Baoyue Huang 👋

PhD student in **Space Physics** at the **University of Texas at Dallas**, advised by **Prof. Lunjin Chen**.

I study **lightning-generated whistlers (LGWs)** in Earth's inner magnetosphere. My work turns the time–frequency dispersion of whistlers measured by spacecraft into quantitative information about the plasma they travelled through and the lightning that launched them, combining **spacecraft observations**, **forward modeling**, and **parameter estimation**.

## Research

- **A fitting scheme for whistler dispersion** *(manuscript in preparation)*: The classical Eckersley relation, t = t₀ + D/√f, becomes unreliable for recovering the arrival time t₀ and dispersion D over much of the inner magnetosphere. Using synthetic whistlers from a cold-plasma forward model, I benchmark alternative fitting approaches and develop a **location-dependent fitting scheme** (by L-shell and magnetic latitude) for more reliable estimates of t₀ and D.
- **Lightning source matching and propagation range**: I match whistler sequences observed by **Van Allen Probes A and B (RBSP / EMFISIS)** to **WWLLN** (World Wide Lightning Location Network) strokes, using the timing pattern of successive whistlers, to identify source thunderstorms and ask how far from the spacecraft footpoint a lightning stroke can be and still be observed in space.
- **Whistler dispersion as a density probe**: I relate the dispersion D, and its change between successive whistlers, to the magnetospheric **electron density (nₑ)** in both simulation and observation.
- **Ducted vs. non-ducted propagation**: I use field-aligned delay integration, ray tracing, and wave normal angle analysis to study how dispersion depends on the propagation path.

## Methods & tools

- **Signal processing**: spectrogram analysis, dechirping, ridge tracing (Viterbi / dynamic programming) for automated whistler trace extraction
- **Parameter estimation**: nonlinear least-squares fitting, model selection (AIC, F-tests), benchmarking on synthetic data with noise
- **Physical modeling**: cold-plasma dispersion, field-aligned propagation delay, ray tracing, ionospheric contribution from IRI
- **Data**: RBSP/EMFISIS burst waveforms and WWLLN stroke data (2012–2019), batch-processed on a remote Linux server
- **Languages**: `MATLAB` (primary) · `Python` · Git · LaTeX
